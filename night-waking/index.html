Night

Toy poodle won't sleep: night waking after the first month

The first month taught them that a noise brings you. This is how the night gets boring again.

Short answer

After the first month, night barking is usually a full bladder, a missed nap, or a habit of getting an answer.

Do a late toilet, check once that they are safe, then stop answering. Sudden waking with pain, heavy drinking, or confusion needs a vet.

Do this

  1. Take them for a last toilet, late, so a full bladder is not the alarm.
  2. Lights down. A chew they can have safely, then the chew ends.
  3. Check once that they are safe.
  4. Then stop answering demand barks.
  5. In the day, protect a sniff and a nap.

This is not the first night

The first nights are in your first night with a toy poodle: one quiet room, a toilet trip, sleep.

This page is for the dog who was settling, and then the barking or scratching starts again after you have gone to bed.

💡 Remember

Bed can be the plan. Plenty of toy poodles sleep in the room. The trouble starts when barking is the only way they get there, and the bark returns every time you move them.

Shouting adds a second dog to the noise. The apartment guide says the same thing about the doorbell. A quiet house teaches the night better than a debate at 2 a.m.

When the night is medical

⚠️ Stop and get help

Call a vet if the waking is new and they are also pacing, drinking far more than usual, sore when they move, confused, or unwell in the daytime. A habit after a change in routine is training. A habit with pain or illness is not.

Questions owners ask

Why won't my toy poodle sleep at night?

After the first month, night barking is often a toilet need, an overtired dog, or a habit of getting a response. Do a last toilet, protect a daytime nap, check once, and then stop answering the bark.

How do I stop a toy poodle barking at night?

Do not shout, and do not start a game. Check once that they are safe, then let the bark go unanswered. If the waking is new and they seem painful, restless, or unwell, call a vet.

Should a toy poodle sleep in my room?

They can. The trouble starts when barking is what moves them into the bed, and the barking returns every time you put them back.
