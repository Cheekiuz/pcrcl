Short answer. A toy poodle puppy's first month is a week - by - week routine, not a night of homework. Sleep, toilet trips, and one quiet room come first. A minute of alone time, brushing, and the harness come next. Barking, teeth, and grooming wait until the third week. You do not need to teach everything at once.

Questions for the first month

What should I do the first night with a toy poodle?

Keep the puppy in one quiet room where they can hear you. Take them out to toilet, then let them sleep. Skip visitors, tricks, and a tour of the house. The longer version is your first night with a toy poodle.

What matters in the first week with a toy poodle?

A sleeping area, a feeding routine, and a plan for the first vet visit. The longer write - up of that first night is the first 30 days guide.

What should I practice in week 2 with a toy poodle?

Alone time first, for about a minute. Then a short introduction to line brushing, then the harness indoors.

When do barking, teeth, and grooming come up?

Days 15 to 21. Open apartment barking first if the doorbell owns the evening. Teeth and grooming at home are the other two pages that week.

I just brought home a toy poodle. What should I do today?

One quiet room, a toilet trip, and sleep. Skip visitors and a house tour. If they are not home yet, buy the basics first. The week you are in is on this page.

I've had my toy poodle for one week. What should we work on now?

A minute of alone time, then a short introduction to line brushing, then the harness indoors. Days 8 to 14 on this page are that week.

Is Poodle Circle a veterinarian?

No. Poodle Circle is written for people who live with toy poodles. These are owner routines, not veterinary care, and not a place to sell puppies. A puppy who is weak, collapsing, or refusing food should see a veterinarian. The longer version is in our principles.
