Questions for the first month

What should I do the first night with a toy poodle?

Keep the puppy in one quiet room where they can hear you. Take them out to toilet, then let them sleep. Skip visitors, tricks, and a tour of the house.

What matters in the first week with a toy poodle?

A sleeping area, a feeding routine, and a plan for the first vet visit. The longer write-up of that first night is the first 30 days guide.

What should I practice in week 2 with a toy poodle?

Alone time first, for about a minute. Then a short introduction to brushing, then the harness indoors.

When do barking, teeth, and grooming come up?

Days 15 to 21. Open apartment barking first if the doorbell owns the evening. Teeth and home grooming are the other two pages that week.

Is Poodle Circle a veterinarian?

No. These are owner routines, not veterinary advice. A puppy who is weak, collapsing, or refusing food should see a veterinarian.
