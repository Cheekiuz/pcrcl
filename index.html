This week’s question

My toy poodle cries every time I leave the room. What worked for you?

Leave for less time than they can already manage. Toilet first, a safe chew, a calm goodbye, and come back before the noise turns into panic. If the crying gets worse as you stay away longer, shorten the practice and ask a vet or a qualified trainer.
