Guide - Teeth

Toy poodle teeth: start before the tartar does

A crowded little mouth collects tartar early. The brush is a habit, not a rescue.

Poodle Circle - Updated 6 October 2026 - Owner routines, not veterinary advice

Short answer. Toy poodle teeth sit close together, so tartar shows up early. Start by lifting the lip and rewarding, then a toothpaste made for dogs on your finger, then a few teeth. Do not use human toothpaste. A swollen face, a loose tooth, blood, or chewing on only one side belongs with a vet, not a harder brush.

Why a small mouth collects tartar

Toy poodle teeth crowd a short jaw. Food and saliva stay in the gaps. Soft plaque hardens into the yellow coat people call tartar. Brushing slows that. It does not treat a tooth that is already infected.

The first month only introduces the idea, in the first 30 days. This is the habit after that introduction.

The habit

  1. For the first days, lift the lip with a finger and reward. No brush yet.
  2. Put a little toothpaste made for dogs on your finger. Touch the front teeth. Reward.
  3. Move to a soft brush. A few teeth, then stop while they are still calm.
  4. Work toward the back teeth over weeks, not in one night.
  5. Aim for a short session most days. One calm minute beats a fight once a month.

What to skip

  • Human toothpaste. It is made to be spit out, and dogs swallow it.
  • Baking soda, peroxide, or a human mouthwash.
  • A hard bone or antler that can crack a small tooth.
  • Waiting until the breath is bad, then brushing harder.
Pain is not a brushing problem. A swelling on the face or under the eye, blood, a loose tooth, or a dog who drops food and chews on one side needs a veterinarian.

Questions owners ask

How do I brush toy poodle teeth?

Lift the lip and reward, then use toothpaste made for dogs on a finger, then a few teeth with a soft brush. Stop while they are calm. A short session most days beats one long fight.

Can I use human toothpaste on a toy poodle?

No. Human toothpaste is made to be spit out. Dogs swallow it. Use a toothpaste made for dogs.

When should toy poodle teeth see a vet?

Call a vet for bad breath that is new, a swelling on the face or jaw, bleeding, a loose tooth, or a dog who will only chew on one side.
