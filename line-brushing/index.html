Line brushing a toy poodle before a full shave

A few calm minutes that reach the skin, so the groomer does not have to take the coat off.

Poodle Circle · Updated 5 October 2026 · Owner routines, not veterinary advice

Short answer. A toy poodle coat mats against the skin, often in the ears, armpits, groin, and behind the legs. Part the coat and comb from the skin out, a few minutes at a time. If the comb stops and will not pass, stop pulling. Book a groomer. Do not cut a mat out with scissors.

What line brushing means

Line brushing is parting the coat in a row and combing from the skin outward, then making the next part right beside it. A brush skimmed over the top only smooths what you can see. The felt grows underneath, and that is the coat a groomer has to shave.

Tools that actually reach the skin

  • A metal comb with wide teeth and narrow teeth. The narrow teeth are the ones that tell the truth.
  • A slicker with bent pins, used lightly. It is not a substitute for the comb.
  • Treats, a stable surface, and a session you can finish while the dog is still calm.
Leave the scissors. Scissors next to the skin are how ears and armpits get cut. A mat splitter belongs with someone who already knows how to use one.

Where to check every day

  • Ears and the flap
  • Armpits
  • Groin
  • Behind the elbows
  • Feathering on the legs
  • The tail
  • Under the collar or harness

A session that fits a small dog

  1. Touch the spot and reward before any tool comes out.
  2. Work one small area, not the whole dog.
  3. Part the coat, comb to the skin, then move one comb-width over.
  4. Stop while they are still calm. Two to five minutes is a real session.
  5. If they pull away, end on a treat and try a smaller area tomorrow.

The first month of handling practice is in the first 30 days guide. Line brushing is that same lesson, once the coat is long enough to hide a mat.

How often

Check ears, armpits, and behind the legs every day. Do a full line brush several times a week. After rain, mud, or time in a harness, check those same spots again. A curly coat does not need a salon every week. It does need a comb that reaches the skin.

When the coat is already matted

If you cannot see skin, or the comb will not pass, the coat is felted. Pulling hurts and can tear skin. Book a groomer. They may need to clip it short. That is a reset, not a failure. After the clip, the daily comb is what keeps the next coat.

If the comb stops, you stop.

Questions owners ask

How often should I line brush a toy poodle?

Check ears, armpits, and behind the legs every day. Do a full line brush several times a week. After rain, mud, or time in a harness, check those same spots again.

What if the comb will not go through the coat?

Stop. The coat is felted. Pulling hurts and can tear skin. Book a groomer. A short clip is a reset, not a failure. Do not cut a mat out with scissors.

When should a matted toy poodle see a vet?

Call a vet if the skin under a mat is red, smelly, moist, or broken, or if the dog cries when that spot is touched. A groomer handles the coat. A vet handles sore skin.
